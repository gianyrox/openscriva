# Short story

One self-contained story, built to protect economy. Three scenes — opening, turn, close — a single protagonist, and one dramatic thread.

## What you get

- **A three-scene skeleton:** opening, turn, close. Enough shape to start, small enough to stay out of your way.
- **One protagonist** in the mind, ready to fill in — the story turns on their single change.
- **One plot thread.** The template deliberately gives you one, to resist the drift toward subplots that bloat a short story.
- **Tight writing rules:** single POV, one tone, cut anything that doesn't turn the story, sharpen the last line. World-building and chapter summaries are off — a short story doesn't need them.

## How to use it

1. Fill in the logline and your protagonist. That's most of the planning a short story needs.
2. Write. When the AI drifts toward adding a second POV or a backstory dump, the writing rules already tell it not to.
3. Use the **critique** panel on the finished draft — short stories live or die on the ending and the economy, and that's what the rules focus the review on.

Keep it to one sitting's worth of story. If it grows a second thread and a cast, it may want to be the [Novel (3-act)](../novel-3-act/README.md) template instead.
