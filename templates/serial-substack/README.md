# Serial / Substack-style

An ongoing publication — a newsletter, a serialized column, an episodic series — where each issue stands alone but they all share one voice. Built so the AI holds your voice steady across every issue and helps you avoid repeating yourself.

## What you get

- **An issue-per-chapter structure.** Each issue is a chapter in your repo, so your whole run lives in one versioned place. A welcome issue plus two blanks to start.
- **Voice as the centerpiece.** The voice profile is the feature that matters most here — your voice is the product, and the AI writes every issue in it.
- **RAG across back issues.** As you publish, the AI can search everything you've written before, so it keeps you consistent and stops you re-running the same idea.
- **Citations on**, for when an issue leans on a source.
- **Writing rules tuned for the form:** first person, a strong first line, one idea per issue, a clear takeaway at the end.

## How to use it

1. Fill in the publication name, the one-line pitch, and — most important — your voice, in a sentence or two. Everything downstream builds on the voice.
2. Write Issue 1 as a real issue, not a table of contents. Give a new subscriber something worth their time immediately.
3. For each new issue, add a chapter. Ask the AI to check it against your earlier issues to keep the voice consistent and skip repeats.
4. Export or copy each issue out to wherever you actually publish (Substack, email, your site). OpenScriva is where you write and keep the archive; hosted publishing to a public URL is on the roadmap (v1.5).

Fiction machinery is off — a serial doesn't need characters or plot threads. If a running story develops across issues, you can turn on plot threads later.
