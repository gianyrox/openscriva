# Novel (3-act)

A full-length novel laid out in the classic three-act structure — Setup, Confrontation, Resolution — with ten chapters already outlined and the fiction "mind" pre-scaffolded.

## What you get

- **A 3-act, 10-chapter skeleton.** Each chapter opens with a one-line beat note (inciting incident, midpoint turn, all-is-lost, and so on). Rename, split, merge, or delete freely — it's a starting shape.
- **Two characters** — a protagonist and an antagonist — with a relationship between them, ready for you to fill in.
- **A central dramatic question** as a narrative promise, planted in Act I and due in Act III, so the AI helps you keep the setup and payoff aligned.
- **Two plot threads** — a main line and a subplot — to track.
- **Writing rules** defaulted for a novel: third-person-limited POV, past tense, show-don't-tell, no head-hopping. Edit these to taste.
- **A voice profile** that fills in as you write, so the AI drafts in your style.

## How to use it

1. Replace the bracketed placeholders — `[Your protagonist]`, the logline, the themes — with your own material. Start with the protagonist and the central question; those two anchor everything.
2. Write Chapter 1. Ignore the rest of the mind until you need it.
3. As real characters, places, and threads appear, add them through the app's panels. Every entry you add is continuity the AI will respect from then on.
4. Set your POV and tense in Writing Rules before you get far — it's the one setting that changes every AI response.

The three-act shape is a scaffold, not a rule. Move the beats where your story wants them.
