# Non-fiction / how-to

A practical guide or manual, structured so every chapter leaves the reader able to do something new. Citations are on, chapter summaries are on, and the writing rules push for clarity.

## What you get

- **A problem → idea → practice arc:** an introduction that states the promise, a foundations part (the problem and the core idea), a practice part (steps, mistakes, a worked example), and a references section.
- **Front and back matter:** an introduction and a bibliography, wired into the book structure.
- **Citations turned on**, with one example entry to show the shape. The AI flags claims that need a source and works from the sources you add.
- **Chapter summaries on**, so the AI keeps the through-line straight as the guide grows.
- **Clarity-first writing rules:** second person, present tense, active voice, one idea per section, a takeaway per chapter, no jargon without a definition.

## How to use it

1. Fill in the subtitle (your promise to the reader) and the target audience — the AI calibrates depth and vocabulary to who you say they are.
2. Add your real sources through the **Citations** panel as you go. The example entry is there to delete.
3. Write chapter by chapter. Ask the AI to **critique** a chapter against the rules — it'll catch hedging, filler, and unsupported claims.
4. Keep each chapter's takeaway concrete: "after this chapter you can ___."

Fiction features (characters, world, plot) are off by design — a how-to doesn't need them.
